This is not a slide idea, it is a running prototype with the full loop. It needs no proprietary data, and the carbon factors come from public databases or are clearly labelled assumptions. On the ground we need a staging area at the guardhouse, a salvage partner, a recycler and support from the Joint Management Body. The four main risks are adoption, contractor habits, safety of used fixtures and small batches, and each has a simple mitigation.
