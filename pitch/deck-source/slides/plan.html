9 / 10

We do not ask for a big rollout. We propose one handover wave in one precinct. Phase 0 agrees the precinct, the staging space and the partners, and measures waste per home today. Phase 1 runs a wave with both roles live and tracks tonnes, skip-bin loads and carbon. Phase 2 extends across the township with real sign-in, reminders and a QR check-in. Phase 3 offers the model to other developers and townships. The timeline is a proposal, so be open to adjusting it with Sime Darby Property mentors.
