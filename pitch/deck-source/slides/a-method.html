How carbon avoided is estimated

carbon avoided = weight × emission factor × condition credit × route credit

A2

This is the full carbon method. Tile and sanitaryware emission factors come from the ICE database version 4, cradle to gate. Other databases give higher values, so name the one you use. The weights per unit and the factors for doors, cabinets and taps are placeholders and are labelled as such. The condition and route credits are assumptions the township can change on the Settings page.
