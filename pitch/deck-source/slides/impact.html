This is a planning scenario from our Handover Planner, not a measured result. We assumed 300 homes, 60 percent renovating, 900 kilograms of waste each, and 30 percent of the divertible share captured. That gives about 162 tonnes of waste, about 52 tonnes divertible, and about 15 tonnes actually diverted, which avoids roughly 5 of 54 skip-bin loads and about 11 tonnes of CO2e. Say plainly that the divertible share comes from a Malaysian housing-sector estimate and that a pilot would replace waste per home with real precinct data. The township gets numbers it can report, residents get free materials and points, and less new material is produced.
