A3

This is the detail behind the hand-off. The system proposes a match. Both residents answer on their own page. The admin can only confirm once both agree, and a second pop-up with a tick box guards against a mis-click. Only then does the item enter the ledger and do points get awarded. In the prototype the two roles are a demo switch; a live version would use real sign-in.
