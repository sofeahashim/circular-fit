Appendix A4 · Data and limits

What is real, and what is assumed

Sources

  • Ain Shams Engineering Journal (2021): housing-sector waste and its reusable share
  • UTHM study: construction waste and landfill space in Malaysia
  • ICE database v4, via Climatiq: tile and sanitaryware emission factors

Limits

  • All residents, listings and hand-offs are synthetic
  • Materials are picked by the user; no vision model yet
  • Demo sign-in only; no real accounts or live backend
  • Route credits, staging size, recycler minimum and skip-bin size are assumptions
  • Used fixtures need a condition check and a no-warranty notice

All data sources and assumptions are stated, as the hackathon requires
