Set the scene. When hundreds of homes are handed over in one precinct, many owners start renovating straight away and strip out sinks, doors, cabinets and leftover tiles that are often brand new. All of it goes into skip bins. The numbers are a Malaysian housing-sector estimate: about 16 percent of materials used become waste, and about a third of that could be reused or recycled. Say clearly that these are estimates. Evidence to add if you can: a few quick interviews with new homeowners or renovation contractors on how soon they renovate and what they throw away.
