Walk left to right and top to bottom. A resident logs an item. We estimate its weight and carbon from a materials catalogue. Routing picks the first route that applies: reuse if a neighbour asked, then salvage through the guardhouse staging zone, then recycle, and landfill last. For reuse, the donor and requester each agree on their own page, and only then can the admin confirm. The match score is a ranking aid: 40 percent material, 25 percent distance, 20 percent wording overlap and 15 percent quantity. The admin makes the final call.
